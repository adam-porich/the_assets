# Human review rubric

Review every cohort item independently, then review the set as a whole. Mark
each check pass, fail, or not-applicable and record the attempt ID.

1. Identity cues — recognizable facial geometry, apparent age, hair silhouette,
   skin tone, and genuine distinguishing features survive. Exact biometric
   likeness is not required.
2. Neutralisation — the source's strong expression, pose, gesture, action,
   scene, camera treatment, and mood have been deliberately discarded.
3. Composition — frontal head-and-shoulders bust, level shoulders, direct gaze,
   calm affect, gently resting mouth, restrained clothing, quiet background,
   and consistent crop.
4. Master quality — broad connected matte planes, deliberate edges, even
   studio-like lighting, restrained texture, and no pixel grid, dithering,
   scanlines, text, border, card frame, or photographic filter residue.
5. Amiga treatment — the 336×276 art has coherent value grouping, readable
   silhouette, restrained background, shared OCS palette, and purposeful
   edge-aware dithering when compared with `target-example-01.png`.
6. Card legibility — the 420×600 card frame, portrait, typography, and identity
   label remain readable at review size.

Reject a draft if it merely filters the source, retains a dramatic source
expression or pose, invents narrative details, produces a generic face, or
tries to generate pixel effects/card furniture. Framing controls may improve
crop, but cannot repair a failed neutralisation review.
